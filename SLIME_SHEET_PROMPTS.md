# Slime art: sheet prompts (several slimes per picture)

Paste one prompt into Gemini per sheet, save the picture to Downloads, and tell me. I cut each slime out, clean the edges and drop it in, the same way as the others.

## Rules that go in every sheet (already included in the prompts below)
- Same look as the Fire and Rock slimes: bold dark-brown outline, simple soft shading, one or two highlights, expressive eyebrows and mouth.
- Opaque, rubbery material, not glass. No glossy sparkle dust, no lens flares, no text or letters anywhere.
- **No puddle, no drips**: each slime sits on a small flat rounded base. Nothing spills or hangs below.
- Each slime stays inside its own cell with a wide empty gap to the next one, on a solid magenta (#FF00FF) background, same size and same three-quarter front view.

## Tips
- If Gemini draws the slimes too close together, add: "leave at least one slime-width of empty magenta between every character."
- If it adds a puddle anyway, add: "the base is a clean flat oval the same colour as the slime, no liquid spreading outward."
- Do the sheet that matters most first (A). Bad cells are fine: I only use the ones that look right, and you can re-roll the rest.
