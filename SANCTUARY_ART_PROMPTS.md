# Sanctuary art pack – prompts

9 generations. Same workflow as the farm pack: paste the MASTER STYLE first, then the prompt. Do all of them in ONE chat thread so the style stays consistent.
Save results in Downloads in the order below (the newest files are read oldest-to-newest, so order matters). Tell me when done.

**Direction decision:** the 20 pixel-art habitat interiors you already have stay. They become pixel "dioramas" you step into, inside a painted frame. Everything new is painted in the farm style.

## MASTER STYLE (prepend to S2–S9; NOT to S1)

> Hand-painted fantasy mobile-game asset, matching a cozy magical slime game. Soft painterly shading with visible brush texture, rich saturated colours, warm light from the top-left, a thin dark-purple outline (#3a2150), gentle rim highlights, no photo-realism, no flat vector look, no text, no watermark. Camera: front view tilted down about 30 degrees. Background: one flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF) colour with nothing else on it, no shadow on the background, no checkerboard, no gradient. Leave generous empty margin around every object, and wide gaps between objects on a sheet.

## What I build with them
1. **Island hub** (replaces the card grid): painted sky, 10 element islands + empty build sites + mist-locked slots, live slimes hopping on each island, gift bubbles floating above islands with gifts, a SANCTUARIES badge showing gifts waiting.
2. **Joy and gifts**: petting, feeding, a friend of the same element and decor raise Joy; Joy fills gifts (goo, seeds, egg shards, hearts, mystery boxes), collected by tapping, capped so you come back every few hours.
3. **Sanctuary Levels 1–5**: capacity 2 to 6, bigger gift cap, more decor slots, element perks that help the farm and battles.
4. **Decor** placed on the habitat floor, used by slimes.
5. **Day/night** tint from the real clock (slimes sleep at night) and the **visitor** who arrives sometimes with a small quest.
6. **Pixel diorama** frame: the existing interiors, with a painted plate, mood icons and a Joy meter.
