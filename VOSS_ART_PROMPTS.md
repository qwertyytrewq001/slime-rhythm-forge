# Voss portraits (for Gemini or any image tool)

The game already shows a drawn stand-in face for Voss. Save a transparent PNG for each mood and it is used automatically, no code change:

- `public/ui/voss_calm.png`
- `public/ui/voss_amused.png`
- `public/ui/voss_cold.png`
- `public/ui/voss_angry.png`

If a file is missing, that mood keeps the drawn stand-in, so you can do one at a time.

## Who he is
A human Forger who went bad. He is the story's villain: polite, patient, never shouts until the very end, and "a lot smarter than he looks". He drains slimes of their Goo with machines and sees slimes as a resource. He is not a monster, he is a tired, precise man who believes he is right. Glim (the golden slime guide) knew him once, long ago.

## Look (keep it the same in all four)
- Dark purple hooded cloak with a high collar, thin pale angular face, sharp chin, tired eyes with a faint amber glow.
- Brass goggles pushed up on the forehead, with teal glass lenses (his Goo machines).
- A small glass vial of glowing teal Goo hanging at his throat.
- A small brass badge on the collar (the badge Glim finds at level 25).
- Same painted fantasy game-art style, thick dark outline and soft rim light as Glim's portraits in the dialogue box. Chest-up, facing slightly left, looking toward the player.

## Prompt (paste, then change the MOOD line)

> Character portrait for a cosy-but-dark fantasy game dialogue box, chest-up, facing slightly left. VOSS, a tall thin human Forger villain in a dark purple hooded cloak with a high collar, pale angular face, tired amber glowing eyes, brass goggles with teal lenses pushed up on his forehead, a small glass vial of glowing teal slime Goo on a cord at his throat, a small brass badge on the collar. Painted hand-drawn game-art style with a thick dark outline, soft purple rim light, rich shading. MOOD: {calm: polite flat half-smile, patient / amused: one eyebrow raised, small smirk / cold: eyes narrowed, mouth a flat line / angry: brows down, jaw tight, eyes bright}. Transparent background, no text, no frame, square canvas 1024x1024, figure fills about 90% of the height.

Tips
- Make the calm one first, then ask for the other three "same character, same outfit, only the expression changes".
- Keep the face readable at about 120 px wide, because on a phone that is how big it is.
- Check each portal's rule on AI-generated art before submitting.
- The game also shows Voss on the dialogue in levels 15, 30, 45 and the final battle (level 50).
