# Glow Slime rework (the Light element starter)

Problem: the current Glow Slime is a golden teardrop with a lightbulb filament inside. It reads as a literal bulb gag and has the generic glossy-glass AI look.

What makes the other slimes work: each one has an attitude and an element-shaped silhouette, with a bold dark outline and simple shading. The Fire Slime has a sly grin, flame hair and a cinder cap. The Rock Slime is a grumpy cube with little horns. The Glow Slime should get the same treatment: a shape and a personality, not just "yellow and shiny."

Lore to use (already in the game): "So bright it technically doesn't cast a shadow, which really confuses photographers." It remembers the lighthouse sanctuaries of the Crystal Coast.

## Style rules for every version
- Same look as the Fire and Rock slimes: bold dark-brown outline, soft but simple shading, expressive eyebrows and mouth.
- Opaque, milky material like custard or mochi, not transparent glass. This avoids the "AI gel" look.
- Few highlights (one or two), no lens flares, no floating sparkle dust, no glowing core, no filament, no bulb.
- Centred, three-quarter front view, soft shadow under it (it can be a small one, as a joke), on a solid magenta (#FF00FF) background.

## Concept A (recommended): the proud show-off
A plump custard-cream slime with a sunburst of short rounded rays growing from the top of its head like a child's drawing of the sun, and one little star on a thin curl at the very top. It is clearly very pleased with itself: eyes closed in a confident beam, or one eye winking, cheeks slightly pink, chin up. The pale cream-to-gold colour is warmer and paler than the current amber. The ray bumps give it a distinct silhouette from every other slime.

Prompt:
> A cute plump slime in the shape of a rounded dome with a flat base, made of opaque milky custard-cream and pale gold jelly (not transparent, not glass). From the top of its head grows a sunburst of five short rounded ray bumps, like a child's drawing of the sun, with one small star on a thin curl at the very top. Very proud and cheerful personality: smug happy face, one eye winking and one wide bright eye, slightly pink cheeks, chin lifted, a big confident smile. Painterly cartoon game-art style with a bold dark-brown outline, simple soft shading and only one or two small highlights, matching a set of other element slimes (a sly fire slime with flame hair, a grumpy rock cube with little horns). No lightbulb, no filament, no glowing core, no lens flare, no floating sparkles. Centred, three-quarter front view, a small soft shadow underneath, on a solid magenta (#FF00FF) background.

## Concept B: the sleepy lighthouse keeper
A tall-ish slime with a small lighthouse lantern perched on its head like a hat, with a soft glow in the lantern only. Droopy sleepy eyes, a tiny yawn, wearing a knitted scarf. Calmer and cosier. Matches the lore (lighthouse sanctuaries).

## Concept C: the dawn slime
A half-dome like a sunrise on a horizon, pale pink to gold, with soft eyelids half open as if waking up, and a few short rays around it. Gentle and quiet, the opposite of the show-off.
