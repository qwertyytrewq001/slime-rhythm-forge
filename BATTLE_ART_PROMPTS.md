# Battle art prompts

Use the SAME chat for all of these so the style stays consistent.
Attach your **food tray image** (the wooden frame with the cream parchment) and one **slime sprite** to the first prompt as the style reference.

Shared style line (already included below):
hand-painted cute fantasy game UI, warm dark-wood frames, cream parchment panels, soft pink-gold trim, clean dark-brown outlines, painterly shading, slightly chunky and rounded.

Rules for every prompt: plain flat solid white background, NO text, NO numbers, NO letters, no shadow on the ground, generous space between items. (Text and numbers are added by the game.)

1) a large wide parchment panel with a dark-wood frame and ornate corners (empty)

8) a thin energy bar frame, gold trim, small, empty

10) a small round close (X) button, wood with a cream X
11) a podium disc, a round stone-and-wood platform seen slightly from above, empty
12) a ribbon banner, cream parchment ribbon with folded ends, empty
No text, no numbers.

## 2. Element badges (10 icons)

Ten round badge icons in the same hand-painted style, each a small gold-rimmed circle with a simple symbol inside, laid out in two rows of five: fire (flame), water (droplet), plant (leaf), earth (rock), wind (swirl), ice (snowflake crystal), electric (lightning bolt), metal (gear), light (sun star), shadow (crescent moon on dark purple). Each badge has a different colour that fits its element. No text.

## 3. Battle arenas (one per region, five images)

Hand-painted fantasy battle arena background, 16:9, side view looking at a flat circular stone fighting platform in the centre-bottom with plenty of empty open ground for 3 characters on the left and 3 on the right. Soft atmospheric depth, no characters, no UI, no text, no frames or borders, nothing painted over the platform.

Make one for each region (same prompt, change the setting):
- Verdant Lowlands: sunny meadow riverbank, flowers, a small village far away
- Whispering Wood and Golden Plains: glowing forest edge, giant mushrooms, windmills in golden fields
- Ironcliff Mines and Murkmire Swamp: red canyon cliffs, mine entrance, misty swamp with purple lanterns
- Frostpeak Skyway: snowy mountain peak, ice crystals, floating islands, storm clouds
- The Forgotten Castle: night, ruined spires, a huge dark castle with a purple glow, lava cracks

## 4. Hit effect sheet (nice to have)

A sheet of 10 small hit-impact burst effects, hand-painted style, each a stylised burst of its element, laid out in two rows of five: fire burst, water splash, leaf swirl, rock shards, wind slash, ice shards, lightning crack, metal sparks, light flash, shadow smoke. Each one centred, glowing, bright saturated colour on the plain white background. No text.

## 5. Victory and defeat banners (nice to have)

Two large ornate ribbon banners, same hand-painted style: one gold and cream with sparkles and a small crown on top (victory), one dark blue-grey with a cracked edge and a small broken star (defeat). Both empty in the middle so text can go on them. No text.
